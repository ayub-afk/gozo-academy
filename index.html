The model

An icon is a brand with an audience and a network. Most of them never extend that brand into a business, because nobody capable stands beside them in the markets where the audience lives. Gozo is that partner. One icon, one project, one network at a time.

01

02

Licensing

03

Consumer brands

Direct-to-consumer product brands built from scratch and co-owned with the icon, launched on marketplaces first and taken into quick commerce and retail.

04

Sponsorship and endorsement

Commercial partnerships attached to the properties we build, structured so the icon and the brand both keep an upside.

05

Digital and content

Podcasts, series and live formats that grow the audience each venture is built on.

Project Uno

Campus Xavi

Campus Xavi brings Xavi Hernández's coaching methodology to young players in India and the Middle East. The programmes run with his coaches, in schools and at partner venues, and the best students travel to Spain. It is the first venture Gozo has built and the template for every one that follows.

  • PartnerXavi Hernández
  • FormatMulti-day football camps and immersion programmes for ages 8 to 16
  • TerritoriesIndia, the UAE and the wider Middle East
  • DistributionSchools, academies, foundations and venue partners
  • StatusFirst programmes in Punjab and Dubai in late 2026
